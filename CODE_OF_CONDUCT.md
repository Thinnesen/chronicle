# Code of conduct

All participation in chronicle (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately to the maintainers. Use the contact in the profile of a maintainer listed in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers act as described in the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
